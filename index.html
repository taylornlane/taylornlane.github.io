Featured Project

Spivvy

A peer-to-peer marketplace for college students to buy, sell, and rent Live on iOS and Android.

I founded Spivvy and built every part of it solo: design, mobile app, backend, payments, marketing. It's a hyper-local, .edu-verified marketplace for college students. Every listing is from someone at your school, every transaction is in person, and every user is verified by their .edu email before they can list or buy. Launched in March 2026 at the University of Michigan, with early traction at Grand Valley State.

Project

My Health Schedule

An AI-powered medication tracker that reads prescription labels with Claude vision, checks drug interactions, and builds your weekly schedule automatically.

A capstone project I built using Claude's vision API to extract structured medication data from photos of prescription bottles, then cross-references that data against RxNorm, OpenFDA, and DrugBank for drug interactions and discontinuation warnings. The schedule auto-populates from frequency text like "twice daily," and a five-week adherence history tracks dose-by-dose compliance.

Academic Project

UN Population Analysis

Data analysis and predictive modeling on UN World Population Prospects data, in R.

A group final project for DATASCI 306 at Michigan analyzing UN demographic data across regions and countries. Eighteen-plus visualizations exploring mortality, migration, fertility, and median age across wealth tiers, plus a linear regression model predicting mean childbearing age compared against the UN's official forecasts. My contributions focused on the median age, net migration, and teen pregnancy analyses, and the ML model.
